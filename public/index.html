    how it works

    Two ways to listen, one engine

    Live joins the broadcast where it is right now — you cannot seek, because it is radio. On demand plays a specific track from the start and rolls on through the library.

    • ★ star a track to keep it in your favourites (no account needed)
    • 🛒 add it to your basket — checkout lands in the next milestone
    • ⇪ share the track page; the audio itself is never shared as a link
    protection

    Streamed, never handed over

    Audio is stored outside the web root and addressed by opaque ids. Every stream request needs a short-lived token bound to your IP and browser, so a copied URL is worthless within two minutes.

    Straight talk: anything you can hear, you can record. What this setup stops is the realistic threat — hotlinking, bots and stream rippers.
